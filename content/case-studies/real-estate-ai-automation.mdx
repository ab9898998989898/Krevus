## The Challenge

A high-volume residential real estate team was systematically losing weekend and after-hours leads to competitors. The data was damning: 73% of buyers ultimately go with the first agent who responds to their inquiry. But with no staff available at 9pm on a Sunday, new leads from Zillow, Realtor.com, and their own website were sitting unanswered for hours — or until Monday morning.

The team had tried a basic chatbot, but it couldn't handle qualifying questions, couldn't understand nuanced buyer intent, and couldn't book appointments directly into the agent's calendar. Leads were slipping through a gap that cost the team dozens of transactions per year.

## Our Approach

We built and deployed a conversational AI calling agent deeply integrated into their lead routing and CRM infrastructure.

- **Instant Response:** The system calls new leads within 90 seconds of inquiry, at any hour, any day of the week.
- **Natural Conversation:** The AI conducts a natural, friendly qualifying call — asking about budget, timeline, preferred neighborhoods, and financing status — without sounding scripted or robotic.
- **Live Calendar Integration:** Qualified leads are offered available showing slots in real-time and booked directly into the agent's calendar before the call ends.
- **CRM Sync:** Every call is transcribed, summarized, and pushed to their CRM with a lead score and qualification status — so agents start Monday with warm, pre-qualified prospects instead of cold lists.
- **Graceful Handoff:** If a lead asks to speak to a human, the system instantly notifies the on-call agent and schedules a callback.

## The Result

From the first weekend after deployment, the team stopped losing leads to slower competitors. The AI agent handled every after-hours inquiry with consistent quality, qualifying buyers that would have otherwise been lost.

- **37%** increase in booked showings within the first quarter.
- Average response time dropped from **4+ hours** to **under 90 seconds**.
- **22%** increase in closed transactions attributed to better lead capture.
- On-call agents reported starting each week with pre-qualified, warmed-up leads instead of cold follow-up lists.
