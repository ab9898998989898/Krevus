## The Challenge

A regional CPA firm was struggling with their client onboarding process during tax season. Partners were spending hours emailing back and forth to collect sensitive financial documents, track down missing signatures, and organize files into their internal systems. The average onboarding time per new client was taking over 3 days, creating a massive bottleneck.

Beyond security, it was terribly inefficient. Documents were scattered across inboxes, and staff spent roughly 15 hours a week playing "email ping-pong" trying to track down missing W-2s, 1099s, and signatures.

## Our Approach

We built a custom, secure client portal designed specifically to replace email attachments.

- **Secure Document Vault:** Bank-level encryption for all file uploads with role-based access control.
- **Automated Checklists:** Clients see exactly what documents are missing based on their specific entity type.
- **E-Signature Integration:** Integrated directly into the workflow so clients can sign engagement letters immediately.
- **Automated Reminders:** The system chases clients for missing docs automatically — not staff.
- **Internal Dashboard:** Partners can see the status of all onboarding clients at a glance with a full audit trail.

## The Result

By eliminating email back-and-forth and providing a clear, step-by-step digital experience, the firm reduced their average onboarding time from **3 days to just 20 minutes**.

- **100%** elimination of unsecure email attachments.
- **40+ hours** saved per week across the administrative team.
- Zero compliance incidents post-launch.
- Increased client satisfaction due to the modern, intuitive interface.
